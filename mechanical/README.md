# Chassis and 3D printing

Teammate owner: to be agreed. Store editable CAD source, printable exports, and assembly instructions here.

Design around the actual four motors/wheels, battery, driver/controller, rigid top-mounted Logitech webcam, and thermal mount. Leave access to wiring, USB, fasteners, cooling, and the motor-power disconnect.

Record wheel diameter, track width, wheelbase, mass, camera height/orientation, and front-bumper offset. Perception needs fixed camera geometry; motor testing needs stable mounts and wheel alignment.

Print a fit-check part before the final chassis. Measure forward drift; equal motor commands do not guarantee straight travel. No steering mechanism is planned. Keep the USB tether out of the wheels and account for its effect in tests.
